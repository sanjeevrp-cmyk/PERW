# Independent Replication Protocol

For high-value evidence, use an independent implementation when practical. Prefer giving the independent executor data/inputs, definitions, Claim Type, Evidence Architecture, construct and sample rules, validation or estimation specification, and causal estimand when applicable without first revealing the exact target result. Compare N, provenance, construct/outcome construction, support, inference, estimates and uncertainty, diagnostics, exclusions, and architecture-specific validity checks. Material disagreement stops interpretation until reconciled.

For high-risk measurement, distinguish Independent Replication, Blind Independent Spot Check, Non-blind Adversarial Audit, and Evidence Adjudication under `protocols/risk_based_measurement_validation.md`. A primary executor may construct all records while an independent auditor targets high-risk cases and material disagreements, supplemented by a predeclared stratified random blind check where necessary. This permits asymmetric effort, not removal of necessary blind review or replacement of an independently specified replication task merely to save cost. A spot check must not be reported as full replication.

Lock blind judgments before comparison; record sampling, exposure, coverage, and independence limits. Include apparently agreed/ordinary cases and raw-source candidates, exclusions, unmatched or unconstructed cases where relevant to detect shared errors and omissions. Agreement is not validity, disagreement is not automatic executor error, and neither agent rank nor a detector score adjudicates facts. Use source evidence and approved rules, preserve unresolved uncertainty, and route protected changes through existing scientific review and PI decision. After construction repairs, trace dependencies and selectively revalidate/recompute results before treating old outputs as current evidence.
