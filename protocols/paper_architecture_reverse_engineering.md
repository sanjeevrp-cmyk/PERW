# Paper Architecture Reverse-Engineering Protocol

Use high-level domestic and international papers as scientific-question, literature, empirical-logic, and exposition benchmarks; they need not be the final submission targets.

Extract:

- question and scientific importance;
- literature gap and residual contribution;
- key construct and measurement;
- data and Evidence Architecture;
- identification, if applicable;
- mechanism and rival explanations;
- main figures and tables;
- Claim Ladder;
- writing architecture;
- why editors and referees may care.

Then answer:

- **WHAT CAN GENERALIZE?**
- **WHAT IS SETTING-SPECIFIC?**
- **WHAT NEW SCIENTIFIC VALUE WOULD OUR SETTING ADD?**

Changing only geography, year, sample, or outcome and calling it novelty is prohibited.
