# Paper Architecture Reverse-Engineering Protocol

Use high-level domestic and international papers as scientific-question, literature, empirical-logic, and exposition benchmarks; they need not be the final submission targets. Verify source identity, publication status, full-text basis, and any formal quality/ranking claim under `protocols/high_quality_literature_anchoring.md`.

Extract:

- question and scientific importance;
- field conversation, closest literature, and residual contribution;
- key construct and measurement;
- data and Evidence Architecture;
- identification, if applicable;
- method, inference, and main threats;
- mechanism and rival explanations;
- exhibit architecture, including main figures and tables;
- Claim Ladder;
- writing architecture;
- why editors and referees may care.

Then answer:

- **WHAT CAN GENERALIZE?**
- **WHAT IS SETTING-SPECIFIC?**
- **WHAT SHOULD NOT BE COPIED?**
- **WHY DOES IT FIT OR NOT FIT OUR CLAIM TYPE AND EVIDENCE ARCHITECTURE?**
- **WHAT ADAPTATION AND ADDITIONAL EVIDENCE ARE REQUIRED?**
- **WHAT RESIDUAL CONTRIBUTION REMAINS?**

Use **literature → generalizable logic → design fit → adaptation → residual contribution**. Changing only geography, year, sample, or outcome and calling it novelty is prohibited.
