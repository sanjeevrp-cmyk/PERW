# Viewpoint-to-Evidence Protocol

Use a scholar's, adviser's, policymaker's, or theoretical viewpoint as a hypothesis source, not as truth.

Map:

**SOURCE VIEWPOINT → CORE PROPOSITION → ECONOMIC / MANAGEMENT MECHANISM → FALSIFIABLE PREDICTIONS → RIVAL PROPOSITIONS → OBSERVABLE IMPLICATIONS → DATA → EVIDENCE → CLAIM BOUNDARY**

For each prediction, state what evidence would support, weaken, or reject the focal proposition and what serious rival could generate the same observation. If available evidence cannot discriminate the focal proposition from rivals, narrow the claim, redesign the test, or HOLD.

The objective is to test the proposition, not confirm the authority who proposed it.
