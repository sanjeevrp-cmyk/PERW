# Stop / Reopen Rules

## Universal HOLD / STOP conditions

- the Core Question lacks sufficient scientific importance;
- the contribution is occupied;
- the proposed geography adds no scientific value;
- the key construct cannot be observed, measured, or validated;
- the data cannot support the intended claim;
- the Evidence Architecture cannot discriminate the focal claim from serious rivals;
- essential data are `TRULY_UNAVAILABLE`: after reasonable investigation, the research team cannot legally obtain them within the research horizon, or the required construct/primary evidence is genuinely unobservable;
- workload is disproportionate to likely scientific and publication value.

## Additional conditions for causal claims

- treatment cannot be reconstructed or has inadequate support;
- assignment is invalid;
- the counterfactual is invalid;
- contamination or spillovers destroy identification;
- a concurrent intervention prevents attribution.

Reopen only when new evidence changes a blocking condition.

An executor's missing login, subscription, 2FA, CAPTCHA, IP/VPN access, connector permission, or similar environment limitation is not team data unavailability and is not a Stop condition. Route it through `protocols/data_access_handoff.md`; keep the dependent Gate pending unless separate scientific evidence supports HOLD / NO-GO.
