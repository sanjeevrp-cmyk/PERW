# Data / Measurement Gate

**UNIVERSAL.** Before full engineering establish unit, identifiers, coverage, construct observability, primary-outcome/evidence observability, measurement validity, missingness, sample support, provenance, team-level legal availability, matching and deduplication feasibility, and reproducibility. For causal designs also establish treatment/control support and treatment observability.

Report source existence, PI/team legal access, executor direct access, delivery status, and validation status separately using `protocols/data_access_handoff.md`.

`ACCESS_BLOCKED_PI_REQUIRED` means `EXECUTION_STATUS: PAUSED_FOR_PI_DATA` and Gate status `PENDING` or `NOT_YET_DECIDED`; it is not a scientific NO-GO. Only `TRULY_UNAVAILABLE`, failed measurement/construct validation, inadequate support, or another verified scientific defect may support HOLD / NO-GO. If independent evidence already supports NO-GO, state why that conclusion is unrelated to executor access.

Use minimum viable evidence, such as a small representative audit where appropriate, before expensive construction. Output verified facts, access and validation states, unresolved measurement risks, support evidence, and the scientific Gate decision when decidable.
