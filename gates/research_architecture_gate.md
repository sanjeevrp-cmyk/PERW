# Research Architecture Fit Gate

**UNIVERSAL.** Determine whether the proposed Evidence Architecture can support the intended Claim Type.

Required checks: Core Question; Claim Type; key construct; required evidence; unit and geography; primary evidence; inference; diagnostics; falsification/validation; main rival explanations; and prohibited claim language.

Output: architecture-to-claim map, unsupported leaps, required repairs, and GO / GO WITH REPAIR / HOLD / NO-GO. Descriptive evidence cannot support a causal claim, and a descriptive or measurement claim does not require causal identification merely for prestige.
