# Competition / Contribution Gate

**UNIVERSAL.** Search relevant domestic and international work, including recent working papers. Compare the closest verified result and its question, constructs, setting, evidence and interpretation with the proposed contribution. Use the existing Candidate Card and Literature Map for the lean comparison and GO / GO WITH REPAIR / HOLD / NO-GO decision.

Eligible routes include a new economic relationship or evidence, an institutional or sample extension with an interpretable difference, improved data or measurement, and independent replication of an important result. State what the target reader can learn about the result's reliability or boundaries, what evidence can establish it, and whether the cost is justified. Original theory and a new method are optional. A change of setting, data or outcome earns its value through that learning. Consider the lowest-cost repair before stopping an otherwise viable project.

At later reviews, identify exactly which finding overlaps which claim and decide which empirical increment remains. Adjacent work can frame the contribution; direct overlap can call for narrower wording, a feasible extension or a different journal. Route material changes to the approved claim or design through the existing authority.
