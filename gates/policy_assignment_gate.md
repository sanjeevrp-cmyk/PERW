# Policy / Assignment Gate

**CONDITIONAL.** Use only when a policy, shock, treatment, or assignment process is material to the intended claim or design.

Recover authoritative sources, announcement and implementation dates, eligibility, treated units, scope, staggered rollout, opt-in rules, spillovers, concurrent interventions, contamination, and selection reasons. Do not call a setting quasi-natural until assignment and counterfactual are defensible.
