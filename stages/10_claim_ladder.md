# 10 Claim Ladder

Validated measure/new fact → descriptive fact → association → causal effect → causal mechanism → external generalization. This is a boundary, not a demand to climb toward causality. Before full drafting, review the lean publication architecture that maps verified results to main-text, appendix/supplement, and replication outputs.

Use the contribution handoff in `protocols/econ_management_publication_architecture.md` to put the strongest supported finding, the exact difference from the closest result, the primary evidence and the interpretation boundary in one place.
