# 07 Main Evidence

Produce the primary evidence defined by the frozen Claim Type and Evidence Architecture before mechanism and heterogeneity exploration. A causal project estimates its primary estimand; other architectures validate constructs, test discriminating predictions, establish robust facts, or estimate their primary spatial/network relation under the appropriate evidence standard. Begin the result registry and provisional exhibit map under `protocols/econ_management_publication_architecture.md`. A materially new estimator, inference procedure, or support-changing architecture requires affected-branch re-readiness under `protocols/method_runtime_readiness.md`, not a complete design re-audit.
