# 01 Problem Discovery & Divergent Sprint

Use theory/viewpoint-led, literature/puzzle-led, policy/institution-led, data/measurement-led, and regional/spatial phenomenon-led genesis. Generate multiple non-equivalent Core Questions, propositions, mechanisms, rivals, outcomes/constructs, and evidence architectures before reviewer-style convergence. Begin with independent scientific divergence; then use `protocols/high_quality_literature_anchoring.md` to map verified benchmarks, serious closest papers, competing literature clusters, and the frontier before reopening or refining divergence. Only later apply a light observability screen to ideas about to receive substantial further effort. Do not let available database variables or existing prestigious papers define what is worth studying, and do not discard an idea merely because its Data Risk is HIGH. Use `protocols/viewpoint_to_evidence.md` for viewpoints, `protocols/paper_architecture_reverse_engineering.md` for high-level papers, and `protocols/early_data_feasibility.md` for the later screen. No formal regressions.
