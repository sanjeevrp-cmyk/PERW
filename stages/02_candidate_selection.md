# 02 Candidate Architecture

Use the current Candidate Card, `protocols/high_quality_literature_anchoring.md`, and `protocols/early_data_feasibility.md`. Compare scientific importance and residual contribution separately from journal prestige, citation count, qualitative Data Risk, workload, and cheapest feasibility evidence. Before expensive commitment, verify the serious closest-paper comparison and the relevant benchmark, frontier, construct/measurement, and method/identification coverage without fixed literature quotas. Distinguish ordinary cleaning or direct-key joins from novel scientific-object construction, entity resolution, historical reconstruction, and large-scale manual judgment. Identify core single points of data failure. When stable output matters, maintain practical risk heterogeneity across the shortlist without fixed LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH quotas or composite scores. Require causal fields only when the intended claim or design is causal.
