# 12 Referee

Run a hostile but evidence-anchored internal review under `protocols/research_director_review.md`. Structural identification failures route backward.

## Reviewers

- **R1 Identification Specialist:** audit assignment, counterfactual, estimand, identifying assumptions, inference, diagnostics, falsification, and threats that could invalidate the design.
- **R2 Field Expert:** audit contribution, closest published and working-paper literature, institutional facts, mechanism, novelty, and realistic journal ceiling.
- **R3 Generalist / Editor:** audit importance, coherence, transparency, evidence-to-claim fit, writing, positioning, and submission readiness.

Each reviewer should provide an independent assessment before ChatGPT synthesizes overlaps, disagreements, and required actions.

## Severity

- **BLOCKING:** invalidates identification, evidence integrity, central contribution, or submission readiness.
- **MAJOR:** materially weakens credibility, interpretation, or journal fit and requires repair or explicit PI acceptance.
- **MINOR:** improves clarity, completeness, presentation, or reproducibility without changing the central design or conclusion.

Do not recommend submission while any BLOCKING issue remains. Route required major design changes through the escalation protocol for ChatGPT scientific review and PI decision.
