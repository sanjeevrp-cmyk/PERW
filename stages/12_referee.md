# 12 Referee

Run a hostile but evidence-anchored internal review under `protocols/research_director_review.md`. Structural identification failures route backward.

## Reviewers

- **R1 Identification / Validity Specialist:** audit Claim Type–Evidence Architecture fit, construct validity, inference, diagnostics, falsification/validation, and design-breaking threats. For causal claims, also audit assignment, counterfactual, estimand, and identifying assumptions under the full causal standard.
- **R2 Field Expert:** audit contribution, closest published and working-paper literature, institutional facts, mechanism, novelty, and realistic journal ceiling.
- **R3 Generalist / Editor:** audit importance, coherence, transparency, evidence-to-claim fit, writing, positioning, and submission readiness. Add an **Argument & Prose Integrity Pass** under `protocols/manuscript_argument_integrity.md` and a publication exhibit/provenance audit under `protocols/econ_management_publication_architecture.md`: check whether empty caveats bury the contribution; process chronology displaces argument; unnecessary hedges or self-criticism add no information; every main exhibit has one primary scientific job; results and headline numbers are verified; major sections have a reader function; primary evidence is central; exhibits, Introduction, Results, Conclusion, and Abstract tell the same evidence-bounded story; Title and Abstract stay within the evidence boundary; limitations are concrete; relevant unfavorable evidence is not hidden or demoted; and results have not caused post-hoc story drift.

These are review lenses. Combine them in one risk-based review and reuse applicable verification. Use independent or blind work for distinct material risks where scientifically required.

## Severity

- **BLOCKING:** invalidates identification, evidence integrity, central contribution, or submission readiness.
- **MAJOR:** materially weakens credibility, interpretation, or journal fit and requires proportionate repair or a PI decision when the unresolved issue matters to submission.
- **MINOR:** improves clarity, completeness, presentation, or reproducibility without changing the central design or conclusion.

Recommend first submission when core evidence, contribution and interpretation are defensible, the refreshed literature supports positioning, and the exact rendered package satisfies the target journal’s initial requirements, including AI policy and disclosures. Block substantive validity, evidence-integrity, central-contribution, or mandatory submission failures. Record nonblocking limitations and carry appropriate improvements into normal R&R. Route required major design changes through the escalation protocol for ChatGPT scientific review and PI decision.
