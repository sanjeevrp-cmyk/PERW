# 06 Data & Measurement Engineering

Make construction reproducible. Audit provenance, units, identifiers, coverage, key constructs, measurement validity, sample funnel, missingness, and transformations. Support text measures, geographic/spatial data, networks, indices, and administrative/firm/city matching when relevant; audit treatment additionally for causal designs. Preserve authoritative links from data and code to machine-readable results for later publication exhibits. Before the first materially nontrivial claim-bearing formal estimation or inference, apply `protocols/method_runtime_readiness.md` where relevant, including temporal provenance and sample-composition diagnostics when support changes materially.

Before scaling unvalidated high-risk core constructions, apply `protocols/risk_based_measurement_validation.md`: stress-test actual unit/event/state, coding, relationship, and matching rules against source-backed typical and difficult cases; reuse valid early tests. Combine targeted independent review with necessary predeclared blind spot checks and common-error/omission discovery rather than mechanical full double coding. Material rule repairs require a dependency/status map and selective downstream revalidation/recomputation; unverified old results cannot support current claims. Existing measurement standards, scope freeze, and scientific authority remain unchanged.

## Standing authority and escalation

| Operation | Executor action |
|---|---|
| Routine, low-risk handling | Codex or the assigned executor may directly repair dates, units, types, identifiers and obvious errors, source legally available fields, and implement ordinary joins and defensible database conventions. Choose and explain conventional winsorization, transformations, complete-case handling or sample rules when they fit the estimand, respect express restrictions and preserve approved objects. Preserve valid zeros and negatives, usable unbalanced observations and missingness. Report important coverage or composition changes. |
| Sensitivity and transparent exploration | Try economically justified proxies, controls, functional forms, sample sensitivities or alternative models in a labeled run. Keep the primary run, all implemented specifications, intention or rationale, and favorable and unfavorable results. Result-informed checks are exploratory. Review whether the evidence warrants a later scientific decision; significance never promotes an exploration to the main result. |
| High-risk evidence or proposed change of a protected object | For unvalidated core measurement use targeted source-based and, as needed, independent or blind validation. Pause only work that would alter a protected construct, main sample, treatment/comparison group, estimand, identification, primary evidence or claim, and seek scientific review and PI decision under Scope and escalation. Other authorized work continues. |

Keep raw data, transformations, merge cardinality and unmatched cases, sample accounting, time-availability checks and reproducible specifications. Ordinary software defaults must fit this paper. A main result already frozen can only be superseded by the paper's authorized decision; exploratory runs are kept separate. Honor explicit task, data-license and access limits.
